Buyer guide

Short answers to common questions.

Silent set or canopy?

Choose a complete silent set if you need a new generator. Choose a canopy if your generator is in good condition and only noise is the problem.

What we need to size a canopy

Capacity, make and model, overall dimensions, radiator side, exhaust outlet and where the generator will stand.

Single phase or three phase?

Small shops and homes often run on single phase. Larger loads and machinery usually need three phase. Tell us your load and we will advise.
